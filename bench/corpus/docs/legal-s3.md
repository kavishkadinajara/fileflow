## Confidentiality

The obligation survives termination for a period of thirty-six (36) months. Disclosure is permitted where required by law, provided the disclosing party gives prompt notice where lawful to do so. Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret and use it solely to perform the agreement.

## Termination and Consequences

Insolvency events give the solvent party an immediate termination right. On termination, accrued rights survive, and the exit assistance obligations in Schedule 4 apply for sixty (60) days. Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within ninety (90) days of written notice.
