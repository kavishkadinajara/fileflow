## Results

eGFR was recorded in 9.0% of reviews in the post-intervention period. Missed reviews clustered in patients booked through the surgical ward on Fridays. Median time to follow-up fell from 108 days to a level within the standard.

### Results — Supporting Evidence

Missed reviews clustered in patients booked through the community health programme on Fridays. Documentation completeness improved by 22.9% after a dietician referral pathway was introduced. peak expiratory flow was recorded in 24.1% of reviews in the post-intervention period. Median time to follow-up fell from 766 days to a level within the standard.
