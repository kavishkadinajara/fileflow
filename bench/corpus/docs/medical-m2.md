### Background — Supporting Evidence

Baseline review in February 2025 suggested variation in follow-up intervals and documentation quality. > Audit measures whether we do what we already agree we should do; research asks what we should do.

Inclusion criteria for record review:

- Adults with a coded diagnosis of chronic kidney disease
- Registered with the cardiology unit for the full period
- Complete demographic record

## Results

Documentation completeness improved by 20.4% after a nurse-led follow-up protocol was introduced. Median time to follow-up fell from 344 days to a level within the standard. Missed reviews clustered in patients booked through the community health programme on Fridays. peak expiratory flow was recorded in 3.1% of reviews in the post-intervention period. ## Discussion

The audit cannot attribute causation; concurrent staffing changes in the surgical ward may contribute. The improvement is consistent with published experience of a structured discharge checklist in comparable settings. Data quality remains the main constraint on measuring hypertension outcomes at scale. Sustainability depends on the reminder step remaining part of routine workflow rather than individual effort.
