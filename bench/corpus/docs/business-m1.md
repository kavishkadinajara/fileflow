## Risks and Mitigations

S. Fernando maintains the risk register; the top item since October has been energy cost volatility. The principal operational risk remains single-source dependency in the retail division. Insurance cover was reviewed and extended to include disruption arising from the warehouse consolidation.

## Outlook

The board will revisit the dividend policy once the analytics practice completes its restructuring. Guidance for the next quarter assumes gross margin holds near 3.7%. Hiring plans in the Western province stay frozen pending the Q1 demand reading.
