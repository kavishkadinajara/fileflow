# Q1 2025 Performance Review — Cendric Holdings

Management attention this period centred on the fleet telematics rollout, which A. Jayasuriya has led since August. Weystone Retail closed Q3 with operating cash flow up 23.7% against the prior period, driven mainly by the loyalty programme relaunch.

## Executive Summary

Cash discipline remained the board's stated priority, and operating cash flow reflects that emphasis. The quarter was uneven: the retail division outperformed its plan while the export markets lagged behind expectations set in March.

Priorities agreed for the coming quarter:

- Stabilise operating cash flow in the analytics practice
- Retender the Southern corridor distribution contract
- Close the supplier renegotiation workstream
