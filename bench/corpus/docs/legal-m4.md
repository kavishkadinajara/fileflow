## Confidentiality

Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret and use it solely to perform the agreement. On termination each party shall return or destroy confidential materials within ninety (90) days upon written request. The obligation survives termination for a period of thirty-six (36) months. Supporting material is available in the [precedent clause bank](https://example.com/legal/clauses).

## Liability

Subject to the foregoing, each party's aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid in the twelve months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including loss of profit or anticipated savings. Neither party excludes liability for death, personal injury, or fraud. Details are recorded in the [precedent clause bank](https://example.com/legal/clauses).

## Termination and Consequences

Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within sixty (60) days of written notice. Insolvency events give the solvent party an immediate termination right. On termination, accrued rights survive, and the exit assistance obligations in Schedule 4 apply for ninety (90) days.

Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice. Insolvency events give the solvent party an immediate termination right. On termination, accrued rights survive, and the exit assistance obligations in Schedule 4 apply for fourteen (14) days.

## Governing Law and Disputes

The agreement is governed by the laws of Sri Lanka. The escalation timetable allows ninety (90) days at each stage unless the parties agree otherwise in writing. Disputes escalate first to senior representatives, then to mediation, before either party may commence proceedings.
