# Q4 2023 Performance Review — Weystone Retail

Working capital tightened as receivables from the Western province stretched beyond standard terms. net promoter score moved to 22.7%, which sits within the guidance range issued in May. Cost pressure in regional operations was partially offset by savings from the pricing review.

## Financial Performance

Cost pressure in regional operations was partially offset by savings from the fleet telematics rollout. Revenue for the period reached LKR 3167 million, a change of 7.3% year on year. The finance team flagged currency exposure on imported inputs as the main sensitivity for next quarter. See the [risk register extract](https://example.com/governance/risk).

Decisions taken by the steering committee:

- Assign T. Gunawardena to the vendor review
- Approve additional spend on the supplier renegotiation
- Defer the fleet replacement to 2023

## Outlook

Hiring plans in the export markets stay frozen pending the Q3 demand reading. Guidance for the next quarter assumes customer retention holds near 18.3%.
