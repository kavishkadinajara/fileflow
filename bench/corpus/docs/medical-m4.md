# Clinical Audit — chronic kidney disease Management, 2024

This audit examines the management of chronic kidney disease in the outpatient clinic against the agreed care standard. The service introduced a nurse-led follow-up protocol to address gaps identified in the previous cycle. Baseline review in December 2025 suggested variation in follow-up intervals and documentation quality. The audit standard requires eGFR to be recorded at every scheduled review.

## Results

Missed reviews clustered in patients booked through the community health programme on Fridays. Documentation completeness improved by 21.6% after a dietician referral pathway was introduced. LDL cholesterol was recorded in 29.6% of reviews in the post-intervention period. The governing relation is $\Delta = p_{after} - p_{before}$.
