## Results

Documentation completeness improved by 9.4% after an SMS appointment reminder system was introduced. Median time to follow-up fell from 685 days to a level within the standard. eGFR was recorded in 32.4% of reviews in the post-intervention period. Missed reviews clustered in patients booked through the emergency department on Fridays.
