# Master Services Agreement between Cendric Holdings (Private) Limited and Almara Logistics Ltd

The Provider processes personal data only on documented instructions from the Client. International transfers require safeguards recognised under applicable data protection law.

## Data Protection

The Provider shall notify the Client of a personal data breach without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two hours. Sub-processors may be engaged with prior written notice; the Client may object on reasonable grounds within thirty (30) days. Details are recorded in the [executed agreement (repository)](https://example.com/contracts/2024/master).

Open points for negotiation:

- The liability cap multiplier proposed by Corvid Manufacturing (Private) Limited
- Audit rights frequency (currently annual)
- The definition of "material breach" in clause 12
- Exit assistance charges basis
- Jurisdiction carve-out for injunctive relief

**Notice periods**

| Event | Notice required | Clause |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Sub-processor objection | 90 days | 11.5 |
| Sub-processor objection | 90 days | 4.5 |

## Termination and Consequences

Insolvency events give the solvent party an immediate termination right. On termination, accrued rights survive, and the exit assistance obligations in Schedule 4 apply for fourteen (14) days. Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within sixty (60) days of written notice.

On termination, accrued rights survive, and the exit assistance obligations in Schedule 4 apply for thirty (30) days. Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice. The full context is in the [schedule 2 — service levels](https://example.com/contracts/schedules/2).
