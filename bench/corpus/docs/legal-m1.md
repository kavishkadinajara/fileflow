## Confidentiality

Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret and use it solely to perform the agreement. Disclosure is permitted where required by law, provided the disclosing party gives prompt notice where lawful to do so. On termination each party shall return or destroy confidential materials within thirty (30) days upon written request. Supporting material is available in the [schedule 2 — service levels](https://example.com/contracts/schedules/2).
