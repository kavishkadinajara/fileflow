# Service Evaluation: a nurse-led follow-up protocol

The service introduced a nurse-led follow-up protocol to address gaps identified in the previous cycle. Baseline review in November 2025 suggested variation in follow-up intervals and documentation quality. The audit standard requires LDL cholesterol to be recorded at every scheduled review.

## Background

The audit standard requires HbA1c to be recorded at every scheduled review. This audit examines the management of type 2 diabetes in the outpatient clinic against the agreed care standard.

## Objectives

To identify process factors associated with missed reviews in the community health programme. To compare systolic blood pressure recording rates before and after a medication reconciliation round. The full context is in the [governance registration](https://example.com/governance/audit-register).

Audit cycle steps:

1. Collect baseline data
2. Agree the standard and criteria
3. Report and embed the change
4. Implement a medication reconciliation round
5. Re-measure against the standard

Reference data lives in the [quality dashboard](https://example.com/quality/dashboard).
