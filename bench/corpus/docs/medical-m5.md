## Methods

Two reviewers extracted data independently; disagreements were resolved by discussion. No patient-identifiable information leaves the clinical system; results are reported in aggregate only. Records of 382 consecutive patients were reviewed using a structured extraction form. The governing relation is $p = \frac{n_{complete}}{n_{total}}$.

Inclusion criteria for record review:

- Registered with the cardiology unit for the full period
- Complete demographic record
- At least one scheduled review in the audit window
- Adults with a coded diagnosis of ischaemic heart disease

## Results

HbA1c was recorded in 30.8% of reviews in the post-intervention period. Median time to follow-up fell from 838 days to a level within the standard. Documentation completeness improved by 12.1% after a medication reconciliation round was introduced. Missed reviews clustered in patients booked through the community health programme on Fridays. See the [previous cycle report](https://example.com/audit/2025/previous).
