## Termination and Consequences

Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within sixty (60) days of written notice. Insolvency events give the solvent party an immediate termination right. On termination, accrued rights survive, and the exit assistance obligations in Schedule 4 apply for fourteen (14) days. See the [escalation contacts](https://example.com/legal/contacts).

## Governing Law and Disputes

The escalation timetable allows sixty (60) days at each stage unless the parties agree otherwise in writing. The agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore. Disputes escalate first to senior representatives, then to mediation, before either party may commence proceedings.
