# Quality Improvement Report: the emergency department

The service introduced a medication reconciliation round to address gaps identified in the previous cycle. This audit examines the management of chronic kidney disease in the surgical ward against the agreed care standard.

## Background

The audit standard requires HbA1c to be recorded at every scheduled review. Baseline review in August 2024 suggested variation in follow-up intervals and documentation quality. This audit examines the management of asthma in the surgical ward against the agreed care standard.

Audit cycle steps:

1. Re-measure against the standard
2. Implement a nurse-led follow-up protocol
3. Collect baseline data
4. Report and embed the change

## Recommendations

Nominate R. Wickramasinghe as the named lead for the next cycle. Add HbA1c to the mandatory field set in the clinic template.

## Governance

Results were presented at the quality meeting and accepted by the divisional lead. The audit was registered with the clinical governance office in September 2025.
