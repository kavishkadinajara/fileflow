## Methods

Records of 557 consecutive patients were reviewed using a structured extraction form. Two reviewers extracted data independently; disagreements were resolved by discussion. No patient-identifiable information leaves the clinical system; results are reported in aggregate only. The primary outcome was the proportion of reviews where LDL cholesterol was recorded.

## Discussion

The improvement is consistent with published experience of a dietician referral pathway in comparable settings. Data quality remains the main constraint on measuring chronic kidney disease outcomes at scale. The audit cannot attribute causation; concurrent staffing changes in the outpatient clinic may contribute. Sustainability depends on the reminder step remaining part of routine workflow rather than individual effort.
