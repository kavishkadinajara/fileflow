## Service Levels

The Provider commits to the availability and response targets in Schedule 2, measured monthly. A persistent failure means three consecutive months below target, entitling the Client to terminate on ninety (90) days' notice. Service credits are the Client's sole remedy for missed targets, save where a persistent failure triggers termination rights. Supporting material is available in the [schedule 2 — service levels](https://example.com/contracts/schedules/2).

## Termination and Consequences

Insolvency events give the solvent party an immediate termination right. Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within ninety (90) days of written notice. On termination, accrued rights survive, and the exit assistance obligations in Schedule 4 apply for thirty (30) days. The full context is in the [executed agreement (repository)](https://example.com/contracts/2023/master).

Disputes escalate first to senior representatives, then to mediation, before either party may commence proceedings.
