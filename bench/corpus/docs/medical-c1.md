## Results

Median time to follow-up fell from 342 days to a level within the standard. Missed reviews clustered in patients booked through the community health programme on Fridays. Documentation completeness improved by 9.5% after a dietician referral pathway was introduced. eGFR was recorded in 25.7% of reviews in the post-intervention period.
